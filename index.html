SVGs exported by Kenney Shape or this editor import exactly. Other SVGs are fitted to the grid, choosing the best shape and colour for each tile.

PNG import

For pixel art: each pixel becomes a square tile. Scaled-up sprites are detected and sampled back to their real pixels. Rounded depth makes the middle of the shape thicker than its edges.

Back and depth

The back mirrors the front, which suits flat things like tools. Characters and objects look better with a back of their own and depth taken from the side.
